# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in Explosion Dynamics Lab to be welcoming, constructive,
and safe.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and
respect privacy and attribution. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or attempts to redirect the project
toward harmful operational use are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates these expectations and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Do not publish private information in an issue.
